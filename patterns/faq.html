Frequently asked questions

Is there a security deposit?

Yes. A refundable deposit is held on your card at pickup and released after the car is returned in the same condition.

How old do I need to be?

Drivers must be at least 21 with a valid license. Some vehicles require drivers to be 25 or older.

Do I need my own insurance?

You can use your personal auto policy or add protection when you book. We will confirm coverage before your trip.

Is mileage included?

Each vehicle lists its daily mileage allowance. Extra miles are billed at the rate shown on the listing.

Can you deliver the car?

Yes. Choose delivery at checkout and we will bring the car to your address, hotel or the airport.

What if I need to cancel?

Trips canceled at least 48 hours before pickup get a full refund. Later cancellations may be charged one day.
